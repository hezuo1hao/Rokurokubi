The Rokurokubi Mod - So You Can Fly!
Added two hotkeys for maximum shenanigans: one makes your head detach and fly around, the other lets you spit to taunt your enemies!

- Fully Multiplayer-Compatible, Built for Online Chaos! Imagine one teammate diligently fighting the boss while the rest are spamming spit and typing taunts in chat. ("Zako~" / "You scrub~")
- Camera Follows the Flying Head! Maybe you should try fighting the boss from a... different perspective?

Let's make all of humanity take flight!

Mechanics:
* Hotkeys
- Head Flight Key: Press to launch your head towards the cursor. Use movement keys to control it; your body becomes immobile during this time.
- Press the key again to retrieve your head, or you can manually guide it back.

- Taunt Key: Press to spit towards the cursor position, perfect for trolling your friends.

飞头蛮Mod，让你飞起来！
加入了两个快捷键用于整活，一个是让你的头飞起来，另一个则是吐口水进行嘲讽！

- 支持联机，为联机整活而生！想想看队友一个人吭哧吭哧输出boss而剩下的人在旁边一边吐口水一边打字嘲讽（杂鱼~）
- 视角跟随头部行动，也许你可以试一试以不一样的视角来打boss！

让全人类都飞上天！

机制：
* 快捷键
- 飞头键：按下后可以将头朝光标飞出去，之后使用移动键进行控制，期间无法操作身体移动
- 再次按下后回收头，也可以手动操作进行回收

- 嘲讽键：按下朝光标位置吐口水，对队友进行一个折磨
